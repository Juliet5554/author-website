Dad’s India — Through His Own Lens

During the Second World War, my father, Peter Merrilees McCann, served with the Royal Air Force far from his Glasgow home. He returned with a photograph album he had arranged himself, preserving the places he had visited, the friendships he had made and moments of everyday life.

Its pages bring together fellow servicemen, bustling marketplaces, a performing bear, and monuments including Qutb Minar and the Taj Mahal. Alongside the photographs are handwritten captions, including “Chaklala”. Some of the handwriting is mine, added when I was a child—a later trace of our family’s connection with the album.

The album remains in our family. Here, you can explore its eighteen pages in the order Dad left them.
