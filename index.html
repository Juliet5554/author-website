It is the history of an ordinary family, told with the conviction that ordinary lives are worthy of remembrance.

The House

At 15 Armour Street, in the Ayrshire town of Kilmarnock, generations of one family made their home. Behind its sandstone walls were lives shaped by love, hardship, war and journeys across the world.

The house is gone now, but its stories remain.
